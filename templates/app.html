Launch App

Everything runs in your browser. No server sees your keys. Dry runs are free — credits are only used on live sweeps. First time here? Read the guide →

Security: This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your mnemonics are never sent to a server. Verify by opening your browser's DevTools → Network tab — you'll see no requests carrying your keys.

1. Wallet source

Connect with the wallet extension installed in your browser. Works with MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Brave Wallet, and TronLink.

For multi-chain EVM sweeps, MetaMask, Rabby, or Coinbase Wallet work best. Phantom and some other wallets only support one chain at a time, so sweeps across multiple EVM chains may skip chains that the wallet can't switch to. For the most reliable cross-chain sweep, use the mnemonic method.

Note: Browser extensions support EVM chains and TRON. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic.

Scan the QR with your mobile wallet, or approve in your browser extension.

Note: WalletConnect supports EVM chains only. Solana, Bitcoin, and TRON sweeping require a mnemonic or TronLink.

2. Your destination address

Where the swept funds land — a wallet you choose. Use a different address than your sweep wallets. EVM, Solana, Bitcoin, and TRON sweeps all deliver USDC to this address.

Solana, Bitcoin, and TRON sweeps are bridged to Ethereum USDC and delivered here.

3. Families

Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic. TRON works with a mnemonic or TronLink.

4. Mode

Live mode: Real transactions will be broadcast. 1 credit will be consumed. Make sure your destination address is correct — this cannot be undone.
No gas? No problem. Empty wallet with no ETH, POL, or BNB to move your tokens? We sponsor the gas for you!. You pay only if the sweep succeeds.
