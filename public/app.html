Everything runs in your browser. No server sees your keys. Dry runs are free — credits are only used on live sweeps. First time here? Read the guide →

Security: This tool runs entirely in your browser. Your mnemonics are never sent to a server. Verify by opening your browser's DevTools → Network tab — you'll see no requests carrying your keys.

1. Wallet source

Connect with the wallet extension installed in your browser. Works with MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Brave Wallet, and others.

For multi-chain sweeps, MetaMask, Rabby, or Coinbase Wallet work best. Phantom and some other wallets only support one chain at a time, so sweeps across multiple EVM chains may skip chains that the wallet can't switch to. For the most reliable cross-chain sweep, use the mnemonic method.

Note: Browser extensions support EVM chains only. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic.

Scan the QR with your mobile wallet, or approve in your browser extension.

Note: WalletConnect supports EVM chains only. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping require a mnemonic.

2. Your destination address

Where the swept funds land — a wallet you choose. Use a different address than your sweep wallets. EVM, Solana, and Bitcoin sweeps all deliver USDC to this address.

Solana and Bitcoin sweeps are bridged to Ethereum USDC and delivered here.
