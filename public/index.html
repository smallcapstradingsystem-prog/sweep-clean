The problem

Existing tools don't solve this:

  • Revoke.cash revokes approvals, doesn't move assets
  • DeBank shows portfolios, doesn't act
  • Zerion / Rainbow work one wallet at a time

Nobody does "sweep everything to one place as USDC."

How Sweeper compares:

  • Revoke.cash → Sweeper also moves the assets
  • DeBank → Sweeper also acts on them
  • Zerion / Rainbow → Sweeper handles many wallets at once
  • Fireblocks → Sweeper is consumer-priced and non-custodial

What Sweeper adds:

  • Every token, every chain, in one pass. Finds what you forgot and moves it all to USDC at a destination you choose.
  • Handles multiple wallets — not one at a time.
  • Sweeps fast in emergencies. If a seed may be compromised, move everything now.

Non-custodial by design:

  • Your mnemonics never leave your browser
  • Every transaction signed locally — no server holds your keys
  • No account, no KYC, no email required
  • Works with mnemonic, WalletConnect, Ledger, or Trezor
  • Independently verifiable — check DevTools → Network yourself

FAQ

Do you ever see my seed phrase?

No. Never. The mnemonic is entered into an HTML form and processed entirely in JavaScript. The signing happens in your browser.

How do I verify that?

Open DevTools → Network. Paste a mnemonic. Click Preview. Search every request for a word from your phrase. You'll find nothing. See why the source is private and what else you can verify.

Why isn't the source code public?

The fee and routing logic is the core of the product, and client-side bundles are trivially decompiled. We've laid out the full reasoning — and what you can still verify without source — at /private-source.html. Security researchers can request NDA access to the private repo.

How do payments work?

You pay in crypto — USDC, USDT, ETH, SOL, or BTC. Send the exact amount shown to the address we give you. Once the blockchain confirms it, your credit is issued automatically. No account, no email, no card.

Which chain should I pay on?

USDC or USDT on Base is cheapest — usually under $0.01 in gas. Ethereum works too but costs more. Pick whichever is convenient; the credit is the same.

What if I don't use my credits?

They stay in your browser until you use them. No expiration, no fees.

What if a token has no route to USDC?

It's skipped. You'll see it in the preview with a "no route" label. The token stays in your wallet untouched.

Can I use this with a hardware wallet?

Yes, on EVM chains — Ledger (via WebHID) and Trezor are supported. Solana and Bitcoin sweeping currently require a mnemonic.

Is this legal?

This is non-custodial software. It does not hold your funds, does not sign on your behalf, and does not provide financial advice. You are responsible for the transactions you sign.
